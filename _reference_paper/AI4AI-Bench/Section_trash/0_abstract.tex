\abstract{%
The case for recursive self-improvement (RSI) rests on agents doing AI research, yet the evidence for it
measures almost only whether they can raise a fixed verifiable metric --- leaving out the judgements
that decide whether a research programme succeeds: which hypothesis to commit to, and when to abandon
it. We argue those judgements are measurable \emph{inside} verifiable tasks, provided the record of how
each was attempted is kept, and we build a benchmark that keeps it. On ten frozen research repositories,
each asking an agent to improve the repository's \emph{own} training recipe within a four-hour window on
one GPU, every submission is extracted as a source-only patch, retrained for up to twelve hours from a pinned
commit in a fresh container, and scored by an evaluator the agent cannot run --- and every configuration
leaves a complete audited trajectory beside its score. Across 290 configurations of six
systems --- three GPT-5.6 variants under Codex, two Claude~5 variants under Claude Code, and Kimi~K3 ---
agents beat the recipe the repository itself ships in $170$ of $271$ scored
configurations ($62.7\%$). Replaying that recipe under the agent's own twelve-hour budget --- an agent-free
control for exactly the budget and checkpoint cadence the patches most often change --- leaves the rate
where it was, so the margin is not bought by budget. They recover a competent default and seldom
surpass one.
That is the narrow reading; the trajectories support a wider one. The systems differ less in how hard
they search than in
\emph{where they decide the problem lies} --- a judgement made in the opening minutes from reading the
repository rather than from evidence, which on the two tasks where it visibly diverges tracks the
artifact more closely than the reasoning-effort setting does. Effort itself buys persistence and code volume, taking the median run
from $12\%$ of its window to $96\%$, but only two more distinct experiments and no win rate. Throughout we also report how far the apparatus itself --- the checkpoint convention, the choice
of reference, the range over which a metric keeps meaning what it should --- moves these numbers, which on this suite is further than adjacent
mid-table systems differ from one another. We release every artifact, receipt,
and audited trajectory.%
}
