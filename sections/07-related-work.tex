\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Reinforcement learning on adversarial games.}
Symbolic construction won adversarial games first, by search over a hand-authored
evaluation \citep{deepblue2002}. Learned value estimation then displaced it: a network
trained on games and refined by self-play took Go \citep{alphago2016}, the human bootstrap
was removed \citep{alphagozero2017}, the method generalized across board games
\citep{alphazero2018}, and the environment model itself was learned rather than given
\citep{muzero2020,efficientzero2021}, following the demonstration that a single learned
policy could reach human level from pixels \citep{dqn2015}. The line then extended in the
directions our suite occupies. Imperfect information was taken by equilibrium computation
in poker \citep{cfr2007,deepstack2017,libratus2018} and by model-free multiagent learning
in Stratego \citep{deepnash2022}; multiplayer and non-zero-sum settings followed
\citep{pluribus2019}, as did games of hidden hands and long horizons
\citep{suphx2020,douzero2021}, real-time control against champion humans
\citep{gtsophy2022}, and games whose action spaces defeat enumeration
\citep{alphastar2019,openaifive2019}. Language models have entered these games as
negotiators beside a planner rather than as the policy's author \citep{cicero2022}. Two
results from this line bear directly on how we evaluate. Opponent-pool construction is a
design variable, not a detail \citep{psro2017,asro2026coevolution}, and superhuman average
strength can coexist with exploitable holes \citep{adversarialgo2023} --- which is why our
test pool is frozen and drawn from a human field rather than reduced to one strong
reference, and why cost as well as strength is a reported axis \citep{katago2019}. A
complementary direction improves the evaluator instead of the policy
\citep{adversarialheuristics2026}. Placing a method within a field of real contestants,
rather than against a single opponent, is also what licenses a percentile as the unit of
comparison \citep{mlebench2024,olympiad2025percentile}. This
paradigm's standing is not in question here. What it does not settle is what happens under
a small interaction budget, which is the regime this paper measures, and against which
learner: comparing a maintained program against policy gradient alone would confound
symbolic against neural with on-policy against off-policy, so the primary baseline is
off-policy and model-based \citep{offpolicyvaluellm2026,samplereuse2022}.

\paragraph{Policies written as programs.}
A policy can be an executable program instead of an opaque network, and this line began by
asking a learner to produce one: a program sketch filled by policy search
\citep{pirl2018}, a decision tree distilled from a trained network so that it can be
verified \citep{viper2018}, a latent space of programs made searchable
\citep{leaps2021,inductivegen2020}, and symbolic controllers recovered directly
\citep{symbolicpolicies2021}. The representation carries a size--expressiveness trade-off
that has been made formal, bounding the size of an optimal programmatic policy --- the
relative of the description length this paper measures
\citep{programmaticrl2024foundations}. Reinforcement learning has also been turned on code
as the artifact rather than the medium, discovering algorithms that were then adopted
\citep{alphatensor2022,alphadev2023}. What changed with language models is who writes the
program: they now synthesize policies directly \citep{llmguidedsearch2024}, search
structured domains such as scheduling \citep{diprl2026,prorl2026}, and supply proposals
that evolutionary selection, uncertainty balancing, diversity preservation, or learned
teachers then filter \citep{qube2024,hsevo2024,teacherawareheuristic2026,
heuristicsearch2026,latentheuristicsearch2026,automodsat2025}. A parallel branch writes
code around a learner rather than as the learner --- reward functions
\citep{eureka2023,rewarddesignagent2026} and the post-training machinery itself
\citep{agent2rlbench2026} --- which is what makes ``then let it write the policy'' the next
question rather than a contrarian one. The closest game-specific systems use
model-controlled program search \citep{codetoplay2024,kuang2025generative}. In all of
these the program is a search output: extracted, sampled, or selected from a population. In
ours it is a maintained artifact, revised in place across a long sequence, which is what
makes its description length a trajectory rather than a hyperparameter.

\paragraph{Self-evolving agents, and what stops them.}
An agent can improve without any weight update, and the literature differs mainly in what
it writes the improvement into. Into natural language: rewritten trajectories, retained
verbal lessons, distilled deployment rules, and refinement from self-generated feedback
\citep{echo2025hindsight,reflexion2023,learningonthejob2026,expel2024,selfrefine2023}.
Into the prompt, evolved as a population \citep{promptbreeder2023}, or into a continuous
text-gradient signal \citep{textgrad2024}. Into the scaffolding, by searching over agentic
designs \citep{adas2024}. Into code: FunSearch established the pattern of model-generated
variants filtered by an external evaluator \citep{funsearch2024}, extended by systems that
make proposal, mutation, repair, and verification autonomous
\citep{alphaevolve2025,avo2026}, by coding agents that rewrite themselves
\citep{stop2023,selfimprovingcodingagent2025,darwingodelmachine2025,mendelgoedel2026}, by
agents that accumulate a library of executable skills \citep{voyager2023}, and by pipelines
that run the whole research loop \citep{aiscientist2024,generativeagents2023}. Recent
benchmarks argue such learning must be measured by transfer and interference rather than
long-context retrieval \citep{agentcl2026,clbench2026}, and how much of a reported gain
belongs to the harness rather than the method is itself now measured
\citep{harnessengineering2026} --- which is why ours is held fixed. Our target is the code
branch with one artifact rather than a population: experience is not retrieved but written
into the program, which is what creates the failure mode this paper measures. The mechanism
is accumulation: revisions leave the program larger and more
interconnected \citep{mccabe1976complexity}, and each further revision must place an edit
without disturbing what already works, until past some size placement fails more often
than it succeeds --- not because the paradigm ran out of things to express, but because the
artifact has outgrown what its maintainer can hold, attribute, and change at once. Each
term of that account is now measured outside our setting. Across 196 checkpoints of
iterative tasks, erosion rises in 77\% of trajectories and verbosity in 75.5\%, at 2.2$\times$
the cost per checkpoint and without matching gains in correctness; human repositories are
not exempt but degrade an order of magnitude more slowly, so capacity is a rate rather
than an agent-specific defect \citep{slopcodebench2026}. Quality-aware prompting moves the
intercept and leaves the growth rate intact, at a cost in correctness, which is evidence
for a capacity rather than a prompting artifact. Two mechanisms underneath are not about
code quality: agentic repair succeeds while the context each step accumulates stays small
\citep{longcontextbugfix2026}, and per-step accuracy falls as steps accumulate even when
the plan is given, though reasoning removes the self-conditioning behind it
\citep{longhorizonexecution2026} --- so a plateau reached by a reasoning agent cannot be
attributed to that effect. A program under revision is the case that cannot be decomposed
away, since the artifact \emph{is} the accumulated context. One boundary keeps these
results from predicting that our loop cannot work at all: the degradation they measure is
driven by a specification that keeps growing, so nothing may be discarded, whereas a
game's rules are fixed and a better heuristic may \emph{replace} a worse one. Accumulation
is a tendency here rather than an obligation, which is why the ceiling is worth measuring
instead of assuming. Frequency and magnitude of improvement do both decay on real
engineering tasks \citep{frontiereng2026}, but those tasks are single-agent and carry
neither a human field nor a learned baseline, leaving the position of the symbolic paradigm
against a learned one open; a survey of this literature organizes it by the strength of the
verification signal the loop closes on \citep{selfimprovement2026survey}, at whose strong
end a game engine with a frozen pool sits. We carry the same longitudinal question into
adversarial games and ask whether the plateau is explained by the maintainable description
length of the policy rather than by the complexity of the game's rules.
